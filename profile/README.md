Latin America's first academic autonomous drone team. Founded in 2014 as a research project at the [Federal University of Itajubá](https://unifei.edu.br/) (UNIFEI), we build unmanned aircraft for missions that rely on computer vision and artificial intelligence.

We compete nationally and internationally — [IMAV](https://www.imavs.org/) (3rd place indoor 2023 and 2025), the [CBR Flying Robot League](https://cbr.robocup.org.br/), and [SAE EletroQuad](https://saebrasil.org.br/programas-estudantis/eletroquad/) (2nd place 2026).
